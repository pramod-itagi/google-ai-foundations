# Introduction

## How to Think About AI

- Treat AI like a partner, not just a tool.
- Don’t just use AI to make slides or documents. Work with it: ask questions, and get different ideas and answers.

## Your Role

- Use your own knowledge to guide the AI and get better results.
- Humans have emotional intelligence and know the situation. If the AI’s answer doesn’t fit, give it more direction.
- Think about who will see your work. Guide the AI so the result suits your audience.

## When to Use AI

- AI is not always best for every task. For sensitive topics like finance, legal, or compliance, using a human is safer.
- For many other tasks, AI can help both with new ideas and work you already do.

## Three Ways to Work with AI

1. **Organize and Understand Information**
   - Ask AI to break down complex topics, find key points, or spot helpful connections.
   - _Example:_ Ask it to summarize industry trends for a project proposal.

2. **Create New Things**
   - Work with AI to come up with text, images, charts, or different ways to say something.
   - _Example:_ Have AI help draft your project proposal.

3. **Improve Your Work**
   - Ask AI to check your writing for mistakes, clarity, or to suggest ways to make your work better.
   - _Example:_ AI can review your sales pitch and spot confusing parts.

## Final Thoughts

- AI is not just about speed. It can help you think better, create with confidence, and make your work its best. This starts when you treat AI as a collaborator.

## Important Note

- Be careful with private information. Never put sensitive data (like credit cards or social security numbers) into public AI tools. Always ask yourself before sharing: “Is this information someone else would expect me to keep private?”
